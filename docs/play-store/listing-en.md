Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing → Manage translations → add English (United States). Copy the blocks below as they are. The app's name on English phones is also **Intern Diary** (`android/app/src/main/res/values-en/strings.xml`).

```

```

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Stop writing your internship logbook from scratch every evening. Intern Diary lets you jot down what you did during the day and finish your logbook in a few minutes, while turning your internship into an experience worth remembering, not just a school requirement.

📒 OFFICIAL NOTEBOOK
• Topic, tasks, check-in/check-out times and what you learned, for every work day
• Ready-made sentences for your field (software, engineering, healthcare, education, business, law, design, tourism and more), added with one tap. Save your own sentences too
• Word goal counter and estimated line count, so you know how much space it takes on paper
• "Copy from yesterday" for days that look alike
• Autosave, so nothing is lost if you close the app in a hurry
• Add photos and choose which ones go into the notebook
• Mark a day as signed

📅 WORK DAY TRACKING
• The end date is calculated from your internship length, start date and working days
• Public holidays are not counted as work days
• Work days and calendar days left, with a progress ring
• Color-coded calendar: filled, missing and holiday days at a glance. Missing days are flagged on the home screen
• Evening reminder so you don't forget to fill in the day

📤 SHARE AND PRINT
• Download or share a single day or the whole notebook as PDF or Word (.docx)
• Send it to your supervisor on WhatsApp with one tap
• Only the notebook text is shared, never your personal notes

🎈 MY JOURNAL: JUST FOR YOU
A colorful, personal space kept apart from the notebook. Most of it takes a single tap:
• Mood, reason tags and how busy the day was
• Moment of the Day: the funniest, weirdest or most panicked moment
• Today's small win
• Song of the day, shared straight from Spotify
• Last 7 days mood chart and a mood view in the calendar
• Internship Bingo, badges and streaks
• Mentor quotes: collect the lines you never want to forget
• Time Capsule: write your hopes and fears on day one, then look back at what really happened
• Internship Wrapped: a shareable recap of your internship

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• No account, no server, no ads
• Everything stays on your phone
• My Journal never ends up in the notebook, PDF or Word export
• Lock My Journal with a PIN
• Back up everything, photos included, and restore on a new phone

Light and dark themes, Turkish and English interface, adjustable text size.

Made for high school and university interns, and anyone doing a mandatory internship.
```
